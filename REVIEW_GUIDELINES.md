# Review Guidelines

Thank you for reviewing *From Competition Problems to Mathematical Proof*.

Reviewers are encouraged to examine a focused section rather than the entire 637-page book.

## Suggested review areas

- Mathematical correctness
- Clarity of exposition
- Chapter alignment
- Exercise progression
- Completeness of solutions
- Accuracy of diagrams and notation
- Usefulness of AxiomTrace
- Suitability for high-school competition students

## What to include in a review

Please identify:

1. Your name and relevant role
2. Chapters or pages reviewed
3. Major strengths
4. Unclear, incomplete, or incorrect material
5. Suggested revisions
6. Whether your name and comments may be published

Reviews should be honest and may be critical. There is no expectation of a favorable rating.

## Reporting a specific error

For a specific mathematical or editorial issue, please open a GitHub Issue and include:

- page number
- chapter or section
- exact statement or proof step
- explanation of the concern
- suggested correction, if available
