## Review 1 — Chapters 8–12

**Reviewer:** Noah H.  
**Role:** Undergraduate mathematics student; independent reviewer  
**Section reviewed:** Chapters 8–12  
**Date completed:** July 2026

### Ratings

| Category | Rating |
|---|---:|
| Mathematical correctness | 5/5 |
| Clarity of exposition | 5/5 |
| Exercise quality and progression | 4/5 |
| Completeness of solutions | 5/5 |
| Usefulness of AxiomTrace | 4/5 |

### Overall assessment

> Based on the chapters I reviewed, the text presents a thoughtful and well-organized approach to developing proof-writing skills through carefully selected competition-style problems. The exposition is generally clear, the exercises progress in a logical sequence, and the provided solutions are detailed enough to support independent study. The book appears well suited for motivated students preparing for mathematical competitions or transitioning into higher-level mathematics.

The reviewer emphasized that this assessment applies only to Chapters 8–12 and should not be interpreted as an assessment of the entire textbook.

### Strengths identified

- Systematic progression from competition-style problems to formal mathematical proof
- Clear exposition and logically sequenced exercises
- Detailed solutions that support independent study
- Strong emphasis on active problem solving rather than passive reading

### Recommended improvements

- Add occasional intermediate steps for readers encountering proof techniques for the first time
- Include additional worked examples illustrating common mistakes or alternative proof strategies
- Strengthen brief chapter-end summaries where helpful

### Mathematical issues reported

No mathematical errors were identified in Chapters 8–12.
